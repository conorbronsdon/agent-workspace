# Decision Log

Key decisions with date, context, rationale, and what was considered but not chosen.

The last column is the failed-hypothesis record: what else was on the table and why it
lost. A future session can audit the reasoning, not just the outcome, and avoid
re-litigating a path already ruled out. Leave it blank when there was one obvious option.
